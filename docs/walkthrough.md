# Inky Bird walkthrough

## New demo film (about 63 seconds, narrated)

The new film is generated by `demo/make-demo.sh` from the real app on a
loopback fixture: a disposable owner, five public approved plates and a
synthetic Garden birds collection made through the real UI. Generation is off
and no sources are configured. UI scenes are labeled **Sample data**. Browser
Play never drives the e-paper wall frame; the film hard-cuts to a photograph
of it on purpose. A silent captioned variant and a caption track are produced
alongside it. See `demo/README.md` for regeneration and review.

### Transcript (new film)

- **0:00–0:04:** Title card, fully visible from the first frame. Inky Bird, a
  field journal for the birds in your life.
- **0:04–0:09:** Slow push into the approved Northern Cardinal plate. The
  cardinal at your feeder deserves a page.
- **0:09–0:16:** Plain-text source chips: iNaturalist, eBird, eBird Archive,
  BirdWeather, BirdNET-Go, BirdNET Analyzer, Bird Buddy. Use one source,
  several, or none.
- **0:16–0:26:** Your journal, then Birds: More plates, search "cardinal",
  open the Northern Cardinal page, Add to Favorites.
- **0:26–0:33:** Collections, then the sample Garden birds collection.
- **0:33–0:42:** Play view: a real Play press, Bird name, Zoom, and a manual
  Next to the American Robin. Plates change once a minute while playing.
- **0:42–0:49:** Manage, then Wall frame: pairing a read-only screen on your
  own network.
- **0:49–0:55:** Hard cut to a photograph of the separate e-paper frame
  showing Eastern Bluebird. Browser Play does not trigger that display.
- **0:55–1:03:** End card: gallery, repository, MIT licensed, self-hosted.

## Previous 28-second film

A silent, 28-second film using the real app, two public approved plates and a
sample Garden birds collection. No personal sightings, production services or
account creation appear. UI scenes are labeled **Sample data**. Navigation is
accelerated; automatic browser playback remains once a minute. It is recorded
and rendered with the commands in CONTRIBUTING.md ("Record a sample
walkthrough").
