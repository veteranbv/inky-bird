# Inky Bird walkthrough

## Demo film (about 65 seconds, narrated)

The film is generated by `demo/make-demo.sh` from the real app on a
loopback fixture: a disposable owner, five public approved plates and a
synthetic Garden birds collection made through the real UI. Generation is off
and no sources are configured. UI scenes are labeled **Sample data**. Browser
Play never drives the e-paper wall frame; the film hard-cuts to a photograph
of it on purpose. A silent captioned variant and a caption track are produced
alongside it. See `demo/README.md` for regeneration and review.

### Transcript

- **0:00–0:04:** Title card, fully visible from the first frame. Inky Bird, a
  field journal for the birds in your life.
- **0:04–0:09:** Slow push into the approved Northern Cardinal plate. The
  cardinal at your feeder deserves a page.
- **0:09–0:17:** Plain-text source chips: iNaturalist, eBird, eBird Archive,
  BirdWeather, BirdNET-Go, BirdNET Analyzer, Bird Buddy. Use one source,
  several, or none.
- **0:17–0:28:** Your journal, then Birds: More plates, search "cardinal",
  open the Northern Cardinal page, Add to Favorites.
- **0:28–0:34:** Collections, then the sample Garden birds collection.
- **0:34–0:44:** Play view: a real Play press, Bird name, Zoom, and a manual
  Next to the American Robin. Navigation is accelerated;
  automatic browser playback remains once a minute.
- **0:44–0:50:** Manage, then Wall frame: pairing a read-only screen on your
  own network.
- **0:50–0:56:** Hard cut to a photograph of the separate e-paper frame
  showing Eastern Bluebird.
  Browser Play does not trigger or synchronize that physical display.
- **0:56–1:05:** End card: gallery, repository, MIT licensed, self-hosted.

The narration voice is ElevenLabs' stock voice George.

The [public bird gallery](https://henrysowell.com/birds) lets you browse the
artwork without installing anything. It is not an interactive controller demo.
